# Universal Transcript DS (outline)

Outline only. Nothing is built.

## What it is

A private DS that reads your Night DS and builds a universal transcript of your strengths: the things you actually did, gathered from your Done notes over a life. A school transcript covers classes. This one covers everything.

## Where the data comes from

- Your Night DS Done archive. Every Done note has its content, its time frame (minute to life), its label, and the date it was done.
- Optional later: Accounts DS entries (Friends, Community, Hobbies) as supporting evidence.
- Read only. Universal Transcript DS never changes your notes.

## How it works

1. **Import.** Add the template, and it reads your own Night DS Done notes. Same personal-instance system as the other DS: only the template is discoverable, and your copy is private.
2. **Scan.** Sage AI reads each Done note and sorts it into a strength area.
3. **Weigh.** Longer time frames count for more. A done decade goal outweighs a done minute task, and many small Done notes in one area add up.
4. **Build.** A transcript grouped by area, each entry showing what you did, when, and its time frame.
5. **Review.** You confirm, edit, or remove every line before anything is shared.

## Strength areas (first pass)

- Education: classes, skills, things learned
- Job experience: work done, projects shipped, roles held
- Strong relationships: friends, family, the people you showed up for
- Communities: groups served, volunteer work, neighborhoods
- Creative work: ideas made real, art, writing
- Body and home: health, savings, care for home (the Avatar's work)

## Labels it reads

Night DS labels map to areas where they fit: goal and lesson point to Education or Job experience, person to Strong relationships, place to Communities, idea to Creative work. Custom labels get sorted by Sage AI.

## Sharing

- Private by default.
- You can make your transcript, or single lines of it, public on your Public Profile.
- Future use: a matching input for housing, jobs, and connections.
