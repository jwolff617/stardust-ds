# Idea Index DS

Post an idea, spark the ones you love, talk them through in the open, and find the people who want to build them with you. A port of the Idea Index prototype (`../idea-index/`) onto Posts.

## What the incumbents do

**Reddit (r/Seattle, r/SomebodyMakeThis, r/startups).** Big audiences, threaded comments, up and down votes.
- Bad: downvotes pile on, the loudest and fastest win, and a good idea from a quiet person sinks in an hour. Karma farming. Anonymous cruelty.

**Product Hunt and Hacker News.** A launch-day crowd and real feedback for people who build.
- Bad: for tech insiders, ranked by a hidden mix of votes and time. "Show HN" is brutal on beginners. Nothing local.

**Kickstarter and GoFundMe.** Turn an idea into money.
- Bad: money first, so ideas without a pitch video never start. Fees. Not about finding a partner.

**Nextdoor and neighborhood Facebook groups.** "Somebody should start a tool library." Real neighbors, real local ideas.
- Bad: the idea gets 40 likes and nothing happens, because nobody can say "I'm in" in a way that adds up. Ads and arguments.

**Idea Index prototype (Sage's).** Ideas with categories, up and down votes, threaded comments, favorites, connections, leaders, and a 3 by 3 grid of panels.
- Good to keep: votes, comments with one level of replies, favorites, categories, the ideas-from-people-you-follow idea.
- Leave behind: downvotes, the leaderboard, and the 9-panel grid (too much on a phone).

## What Stardust does differently

1. **Spark, not vote up and down.** One tap says "I love this." No downvotes: in a small community they're used to pile on, and reports handle what's truly bad. Sparks are counted on the idea, never on people.
2. **I'm in.** The step every other site misses. Tap "I'm in," say what you'd bring ("I can do design," "I have a garage"), and your name shows on the idea with the others who said yes. The poster sees the team forming and messages people privately.
3. **Three kinds.** Idea (a thought that could be built), Building (we're making it, come help), Need (a problem looking for an idea). A Need can turn into an Idea, and an Idea into Building.
4. **Every idea names a first small step.** "Ask the library about a room." Small steps are how ideas leave the page.
5. **Open conversation.** Comments are public with one level of replies, so the thinking stays readable. Private messages are still there for the details.
6. **No leaderboard.** Sort by newest or by most sparked, never by who's "top." Quiet ideas get a fair start because new ones show first.
7. **Ideas don't expire.** Needs last 90 days unless renewed; Ideas and Building stay until closed.

## Kinds and fields

| Kind | Its own fields | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | The problem it solves, First small step, Looking for (who'd help) | Until closed |
| Building | Stage (just starting, making it, up and running), Looking for, How to join | Until closed |
| Need | Who has this need, What would help | 90 days |

Every post has a title, details, neighborhood, and up to 10 labels (the prototype's categories become labels).

**Labels suggested:** housing, education, transportation, community, food, art, music, tech, health, environment, business, kids, games, writing, sports, nonprofit, seattle.

## The words

| Term | Part | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | more | A thought that could be built. Says the problem it solves and a first small step. |
| Building | more | An idea people are making now, and how to come help. |
| Need | more | A problem looking for an idea. Say who has it and what would help. |
| Spark | verb | Tap to say you love an idea. One per spark per idea, and no downvotes. |
| I'm In | verb | Say you'd help build an idea, and what you'd bring. Your name shows on it with the others who said yes. |
| Comment | noun | An open note on an idea that anyone on the board can read, with one level of replies. |
| First Small Step | noun | The smallest real thing that moves an idea off the page. |

## Safety and operations

- Comments: Report (with a reason) on every comment. Three reports hide a comment until a steward looks; stewards see hidden comments in Review and clear or remove them. Delete your own comment any time.
- Blocks apply to comments and I'm In too: you don't see a blocked spark's comments, and they don't see yours.
- Rate limits: 30 comments an hour, plus the shared 10 posts a day.
- Ideas are public to every signed-in spark. The form says so: post what you're glad for others to build on. Stardust doesn't police "stealing ideas"; the poster's name and date are on it.
- Money: Stardust handles none. If a team wants to raise money, that happens off the board (Idea House is one route for small in-kind grants).

## Sage AI

## Real people, walked through

**Denise, Recovery Cafe member.** She posts a Need: "A place to charge phones overnight near First Hill." Who has it: people sleeping outside. What would help: a locker with an outlet. Labels housing, community. Two comments come in, one from a SHARE participant who says Lockers has outlets. Someone else turns it into an Idea: "Charging lockers at the Cafe," with a first step of asking the Cafe director.
- Check: Need fields work; the conversation stays readable with one level of replies; nothing ranks her below anyone.

**Marcus, SHARE participant, library computer.** He sparks three ideas and taps "I'm in" on "Free bike repair Saturdays in Lake City," offering "I fixed bikes for 5 years." His name shows on the idea. The poster messages him. Saved keeps the ideas he's watching.
- Check: I'm In shows names and offers; private messages still work; it's all on a library computer.

**Priya, YMCA staff.** She posts Building: "Kids' board game swap at the Y." Stage just starting. Looking for: volunteers on Saturdays. How to join: say I'm in or message me. Six people say they're in. She closes it when the swap is running, and posts a new Building for the next one.

**Tom, Ballard neighbor.** He sorts by Most sparked to see what the neighborhood loves, filters #transportation, and sparks a bike-lane idea.

## How it grows

1. **Sage's own circle first** (Idea House applicants, Urban Wolf readers). The board needs ideas on day one: Sage and the first few people post the ideas already in `Evolve_Idea_Index_Dictionary.md` and the old Idea Index.
2. **Recovery Cafe and SHARE:** Needs, which turn into Ideas. A steward from each.
3. **Idea House link:** a Building idea that needs materials can apply to the Idea House Fund (later: a button that starts an application from the idea).
4. **Neighborhoods** join as people post local ideas.
