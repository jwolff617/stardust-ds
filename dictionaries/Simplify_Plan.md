# Simplify Stardust DS (2026-11-19)

**Stardust DS: write what's on your mind, find your words, meet people who share them.**

Claude and ChatGPT handle money and the big tasks. Stardust is the small, quiet place for a clear mind and real connection. It doesn't run on money: Stardust never moves money, never sells anything, and has no ads, feed, likes, or follower counts. It's free for nonprofits and neighbors, and it starts in Seattle.

## The whole dictionary

**Verbs:** Write, Match.

**Nouns:** Note, Journal, Person, Idea, Resource, Job.

**Spark:** a person or an organization.

## Write

**Private.** Nobody sees these.

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notepad** | Notes that stay until you delete them |
| **Night Notes** | Notes with a time frame that let go when it's up (Night DS's notes) |
| **Journal** | Dated entries, newest first |

To make a new notebook, pick one of the three and name it. That replaces general dictionary creation.

Also private: Address Book (with birthdays), Resources you keep private, and Sage's own notebooks (Worthy, Hazel Valley).

**Public.** This is what matches read.

| You write | Organizations write | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| **Me**: who you are, in your words (was Manifesto) | (no) | Person Match |
| **My Ideas** | Their ideas | Idea Match |
| **My Resources**: things you Have or Need | Their resources: free lunch, showers, lockers, classes | Resource Match |
| **My Work**: the work you love and want | Open jobs | Job Match |

## Match

All four matches work the same way:

1. Yours is at the top: write it or edit it.
2. Matches appear underneath, showing the shared words ("you both care about play, kids, outdoors").
3. To start talking, you tap Say Hello. Block and Report are on every match.

- **Person Match:** Friends, Business Partners, Dating (18+, mutual, one per person, as built).
- **Idea Match:** was Idea Index.
- **Resource Match:** was Marketplace, with no prices. Have or Need: give, lend, swap, borrow, ask. A help line sits at the top: 988 and shelters.
- **Job Match:** paid jobs are welcome, and each job shows its pay. Matching is only on passion fit, never pay: nothing sorts, filters, or ranks by pay. WA law requires a pay range on postings from employers with 15 or more employees, so the job form keeps a pay line.

## Organizations and Official

- An organization signs up as an organization and can write resources, ideas, and jobs right away.
- **Apply for Official** moves off Business Pages onto the organization's account. Sage approves or sends it back, reusing the draft / applied / official flow from 0032.
- Official organizations show first and carry the tag. Anyone can filter to Official only.
- Jobs from organizations that aren't Official show "Not checked by Stardust yet."
