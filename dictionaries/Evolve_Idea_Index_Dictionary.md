# Evolve / Idea Index — Dictionary

Not part of the STARDUST Dictionary book. This is a topical dictionary Urban Wolf Studio offers as an uploader on **Stardust DS** — the platform where non-profits and businesses upload, share, and search their own dictionaries. It's project and business-idea vocabulary, not feeling/body/spirit/soul vocabulary, so it lives here instead of in the core Stardust Dictionary. Previously lived in the STARDUST master file; per Sage's direction this content belongs in Urban Wolf Studio's coding/apps track, not the dictionary book.

Pulled out of the old STARDUST master dictionary file on 2026-08-10, content unchanged.

---

**Ideas Index** — A living repository of ideas that want to exist. Not a to-do list — a garden. Ideas get planted, watered, and either grow or don't. The purpose is not completion. The purpose is keeping ideas alive long enough for the right person to find them. Can receive public contributions — up/down voted, with stories attached.

**Retirement Casino** — A gambling establishment where 80% of every bet — win or lose — goes directly into the bettor's retirement account. 10% to the operator, 10% to bonus payouts. Turns the addiction into savings. Gets millions of gambling-addicted people out of poverty. Requires legislation.

**Love Lotto** — A dating lottery. Everyone has a number. The lottery finds your match — not by algorithm, but by chance and category. The point: awareness that love is everywhere, in everyone, and sometimes you just need the lottery to show you who to look at.

**Venus Dating App** — A dating app built around what happened on your last date — your thoughts, your feelings, your desires, what you learned about yourself. The app matches people not by profile but by how honestly they can talk about connection.

**Teen Council** — A student government with real authority and a real budget, integrated into city government. Executive, legislative, judicial branches. Student constitution. Real votes that go to city hall. Young people as genuine civic participants now, not future voters.

**Bet Stories** — The principle: do not hedge everything. Accept losing as a real possibility before you bet. Bet an affordable amount to lose, then work backward. Good odds already price in the randomness and chaos that makes games interesting.

**Night Librarian** — A librarian on duty at 2am for children who wake up wondering. The city needs to be safe enough that a child can walk down the sidewalk at night and find someone to answer their questions. The Night Librarian is the test of whether a city truly loves its children.

**Empathy Foundation** — An organization that funds love. Grants for tents, sporting goods, games, art supplies, after-school programs, housing. Measures success not in outputs but in empathy demonstrated. Businesses receive the Stardust badge if they follow love-first principles.

**Bunkhouse Industries** — A holding company that invests in homeless-led businesses and gives those leaders real ownership. Food trucks, cafes, animation studios. The Berkshire Hathaway of the people who need a chance.

**Stardust Badge** — A mark of endorsement. Given to businesses that follow Stardust values: love, honesty, community. Controlled by the brand. Cannot be bought — only earned.

**Community Center** — A city-managed block containing: park, library, museum, after-school program, apartment building, homeless-run coffee shop, storage lockers, bus stop. Available 24 hours. The model for what a city block can be when designed around people instead of cars.

**Empathy Squared (222)** — The grant program and community philosophy. Grants of $50 to $50,000 for business startups, toys and sports, housing, development. Requires recipients to follow love-based operating principles. Grant amounts and impact tracked, shared publicly.

**The Orgasm Book** — A short standalone companion to WACK. Instructions only. No lecture. The golden glow as proof of concept. More details about amazing orgasms — because people aren't having them, and the reason is they don't have the golden glow. Can be WACK Part II or a separate book entirely. The most practical guide ever written.

**City Co** — A comedy TV show. The premise: people think they are living on a boy alien in a coma. They are actually aliens on a different planet, in a different star system — not Earth. The planet has one world government. Three different resources instead of water, wood, and steel. The joke is stock markets. People doing jobs. Typing on computers. It is hilarious. The real world underneath: electricity, dreams, the origin, the DNA.
